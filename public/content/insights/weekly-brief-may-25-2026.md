## 12. Weekly Insight

> "Capital does not follow price. Price follows capital."

This week's core driver was a **reversal in flow direction**, not price itself. Nine straight days of BTC ETF outflows weakened the core institutional spot bid, translating into the joint weakness of BTC −5.1% and ETH −5.6%. A hawkish Fed (2Y 3.99%, 10Y 4.45%) and a near-5% 30Y raised the opportunity cost of non-yielding assets as a structural drag.

Beneath the surface weakness, **fundamentals diverged**. On-chain credit/RWA names (Maple +48.3%, Morpho +44.2%, MakerDAO +42.1%) posted double-digit fee growth, differentially attracting capital to protocols generating real yield even in a downtape — a sign of maturation from "narrative bets" to "cash-flow bets."

The AI×DePIN counter-trend strength also stands out. Price strength in WLD (+12.91%) and VVV (+10.92%) coincided with real-revenue growth at Aethir (+110% fees), confirming AI-infra demand on both price and fundamentals. Which sector runs counter when the broad market is weak often hints at the next cycle's leadership.

Rotation into emerging L1s (HyperEVM +13.3%, Aptos +11.5% TVL) and outflows from some chains (Avalanche −14.6%, Starknet −22.9%) suggest a deepening winner-take-most structure within L1/L2.

**Allocation note (informational, not advice):** In a weak, high-rate regime, reasonable approaches include (1) tilting toward real-yield RWA/credit protocols, (2) phased management of BTC beta until ETF re-inflows confirm, and (3) small watchlist exposure to counter-trend sectors like AI×DePIN.
