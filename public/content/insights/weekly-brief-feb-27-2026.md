## 1. In-depth Analysis of Macroeconomic Indicators and Macro Environment

In late February 2026, the global macroeconomic environment is experiencing extreme asset repricing and heightened volatility, driven by an intersection of escalating geopolitical tensions and the radical trade protectionism policies of the U.S. administration. In particular, uncertainties surrounding tariff policies are stimulating fears of a resurgence in inflation, acting as a core driver sharply elevating risk premiums across both traditional financial markets and the broader digital asset market.

### 1.1. U.S. Tariff Shock and the Structural Response of the Bond Market

On February 20, 2026, the U.S. Supreme Court ruled the so-called "Liberation Day" tariff measures pushed by the Trump administration as unconstitutional, forming a temporary relief rally in the market. However, this relief did not last a single day. President Trump immediately invoked Section 122 of the 1974 Trade Act, announcing the sudden imposition of a 15% global temporary tariff applicable for up to 150 days unless extended by Congress. Concurrently, the European Parliament froze the approval process for a trade agreement between the U.S. and the EU, and in retaliation, the U.S. administration threatened a 200% tariff on French wines and champagnes. As trade disputes across the Atlantic reached extremes, fears of global supply chain impairment peaked.

> This intensification of trade wars strongly stimulates concerns over a recurrence of "Cost-push Inflation" via rising import prices, emerging as the greatest macroeconomic risk that could structurally delay the Federal Reserve's monetary policy normalization (rate cut) cycle. The bond market immediately reflected these fears of entrenched inflation.

Bitcoin Dominance, which represents **Bitcoin**'s market share within the total cryptocurrency market, maintains a record high of **58.59%** as of Feb 27. According to TradingView data, this is close to a multi-year high. Such high dominance clearly illustrates that under maximized macro uncertainty, institutional and retail investors are actively reducing portfolio exposure to illiquid and highly volatile altcoins, opting instead for a massive "Flight to Quality" into Bitcoin, which is evaluated as the safest digital Store of Value.

Funding Rates, which gauge the degree of speculative overheating in the derivatives market, also distinctly reflect the ongoing de-risking in the market. According to VanEck research, perpetual futures funding rates for Ethereum (ETH) and Solana (SOL) flipped into negative territory, and Bitcoin funding rates aggressively compressed to the zero (0) level. Unlike past crashes where shorts bet aggressively downward driving funding rates extremely negative, this signifies a healthy "Position Reduction" phase—excessive long positions were liquidated en masse, and market participants have drastically lowered their leverage to avoid risk.

The Kimchi Premium (the price gap between Korean KRW exchanges like Upbit and global exchanges), a unique indicator of the Korean crypto market and a proxy for Asian retail sentiment, provides a highly interesting signal. After hovering around zero or negative (discount) for several months, the Kimchi Premium turned positive again starting mid-February, generating a so-called "Long Signal." According to an on-chain analysis report by decentralized asset management platform XWIN Finance, the phenomenon of the Kimchi Premium raising its head to positive after prolonged stagnation historically implies that strong spot buying demand, centered on Korean retail investors, is locally aggregating. This is highly significant as past data shows this phenomenon repeatedly acting as a leading indicator observed during bottom-building phases right before massive global Bitcoin market rallies.

### 2.3. On-chain Valuation: Macro Implications of MVRV, NUPL, and S2F Models

Long-term valuation indicators embedded in on-chain data prove from multiple angles that the current market collapse is, in fact, an attractive accumulation opportunity.

- **Rolling MVRV Z-score:** Based on methodologies from Glassnode and MacroMicro, Bitcoin's MVRV Z-Score recorded 0.45 as of Feb 25. The MVRV index represents the standard deviation of the gap between Bitcoin's Market Cap and its Realized Cap (the sum of the price of all coins when they last moved on-chain, proxying the average purchase price). The compression of this figure to 0.45 numerically confirms that Bitcoin's current market price (near $67,000) shows almost no difference from the global average on-chain purchase price, meaning it has entered a perfectly bubble-free "Green Zone" (undervalued territory). In past cycles, whenever this indicator fell below or approached 0, it was without exception the bottom of the cycle and a precursor to an explosive bull market.
- **NUPL Index (Net Unrealized Profit/Loss):** Glassnode's NUPL indicator, measuring the ratio of net unrealized profit to loss across all network participants, shows stark temperature differences by asset, reflecting the maturity of each ecosystem.

- **BTC NUPL (0.196):** Bitcoin shows a fairly compressed figure of 0.196. While the tail ends of severe bear markets in the past were accompanied by capitulation phenomena where this figure dropped below zero, it is currently supported with profitable holdings still slightly outnumbering holdings in loss. This implies that the market is not in a panic phase with massive stop-losses pouring out, but rather in a "Stress" (pain endurance) zone where Long-term Holders are gripping their coins while enduring the price decline in the latter half of the tightening cycle.
- **ETH NUPL (-0.1689):** According to CryptoQuant analysis, Ethereum's NUPL—specifically measured within the internal wallets of Binance, the world's largest exchange—plummeted to -0.1689, renewing a 9-month low. A negative NUPL figure ruthlessly demonstrates that a significant portion of the Ethereum volume currently held on Binance is in an unrealized loss state, trapped at the top. However, because investors historically exhibit an extreme reluctance to sell in heavy loss zones (Disposal Effect), CryptoQuant evaluates that the current zone has entered a **"Potential Accumulation Zone"** where powerful potential selling pressure is effectively exhausted, and quiet accumulation by whales is occurring.
- **SOL NUPL (-0.0579):** Solana's NUPL entered a clear net loss zone, recording -0.0579. This shows that investors in the Solana network have absorbed on-chain losses following the recent plunge after last autumn's surge, placing them at the tail end of a typical "Capitulation" phase. However, as mentioned earlier, Solana is showing relatively superior price defense amidst macro risks, hinting that this capitulation zone could rather be a mid-to-long-term entry timing.

**Stock-to-Flow (S2F) Model Divergence:** From the perspective of the traditional Stock-to-Flow model, which calculates long-term intrinsic value based on Bitcoin's scarcity, the current Bitcoin price (around $67,454) is trading at a significantly lower level than the trajectory predicted by the model post-halving. This is interpreted as the fundamental change of the sharp supply reduction not yet being fully priced into the market, suggesting a state of severe undervaluation persists on a long-term time series.
