# Bitcoin 4-Year Halving Cycle Analysis — How Far Could This Decline Go? (As of June 5, 2026)

**By Nexus One Research Desk • June 5, 2026 • 25 min read**

---

## TL;DR

- Bitcoin fell from its $126,198 top on October 6, 2025 to roughly $62,000 by June 2026, a drawdown of about **-49.5 to -51%**. That's shallower than the three prior major bear markets (-77 to -93%) but nearly identical to the May 2021 mid-cycle correction (-53%). The market is split exactly in two over whether this is a "cycle-ending bear market" or a "mid-bull-market correction" — and which one it is **has not yet been settled**.
- Applying past bear-market averages, the downside target is \~$29,000 at -77% and \~$20,200 at -84%. Viewed as a mid-cycle correction, the floor is the -50 to -53% zone (already reached) around $59,000–$63,000. The most trusted on-chain supports are the **200-week moving average \~$61,000** (already touched June 4), **total realized price \~$54,000**, and **long-term holder cost basis \~$48,000**.
- The view that the 4-year cycle has been weakened/killed by ETFs and institutional capital (Bitwise, CryptoQuant CEO) clashes with the view that it still holds (PlanB, parts of Fidelity). The shallower-than-usual decline is a point for the bulls, but the CryptoQuant CEO warns the bear market could run into early 2027.

## Key Findings

1. **Current position (fact):** \~777 days (25.5 months) elapsed from the April 19/20, 2024 halving to now (2026-06-05). The top formed \~534 days after the halving (2025-10-06) — longer than the historical average (481 days) and similar to 2021 (549 days).
2. **Magnitude of decline (fact):** The current -49.5 to -51% drawdown is shallower than the three prior major bears (-86%, -84%, -77%) and nearly identical to the May 2021 mid-cycle correction (-53%).
3. **Core debate (estimate):** Views split three ways — (a) cycle-ending bear market, (b) mid-cycle correction, (c) a "10-year grind" structural shift.
4. **On-chain supports (fact + estimate):** 200-week MA \~$61,000 (touched $61,300 on June 4), realized price \~$54,000, long-term holder cost basis \~$48,000.
5. **Bear-market-bottom signal flashing (fact):** June 4, 2026, CoinDesk (citing Glassnode) — "10.5M BTC in loss vs. 9.8M in profit." Loss-supply overtaking profit-supply is a hallmark of every past bear-market bottom.

- **Avg. halving → top: 481 days** (first three cycles). In every cycle, the top forms 12–18 months after the halving.

## 2. Current Cycle Position Analysis

- **Elapsed time (fact):** 2024-04-20 halving → 2026-06-05 ≈ 777 days (25.5 months).
- **Comparison to past at the same point (fact):** Past cycles topped \~18 months (525–549 days) after the halving, then fell sharply for 6–9 months. This cycle also topped \~534 days after the halving (2025-10-06) and has been declining for \~8 months — which lines up precisely with the early-to-mid stage of past bear markets.
- **Historical anomaly in top timing (fact):** This cycle set an all-time high of $73,581 on March 14, 2024 — *before* the halving, a first in history. The spot ETFs approved in January 2024 pulled demand forward (CoinGecko, Grayscale). In the past, prior highs were only reclaimed at least 12 months after the halving.

**Analyst views are split (estimates):**

- **Mid-cycle correction camp:** CoinDesk (2026-01-10) — the -36% drop in the 90 days after the top is shallower than past cycles (2014 -71%, 2017 -70%, 2021 -51%), fitting a mid-cycle correction. K33 — the \~$60,000 low in February 2026 (-52% from $126,272) may already be this cycle's maximum drawdown.
- **Bear market camp:** CryptoQuant CEO Ki Young Ju (2026-05-29, X, verbatim) — *"Once profit-taking cascades, Bitcoin investors' PnL typically falls for about 18 months. Since the trend turned in Oct 2025, the bear market could last until early 2027. The trend only changes when unrealized profits rise and realized profits fall. We're not there yet."*
- **Structural change camp:** Bitwise's Matt Hougan (2025-12-26 memo, verbatim) — *"We're not in a four-year cycle anymore. We're in a 10-year grind upward with strong returns and lower volatility."* (Memo title: "The Four-Year Cycle Is Dead. Welcome to the Ten-Year Grind.")

## 3. Historical Mid-Cycle Correction Cases (Facts)

- **2017:** 13 separate -30 to -40% corrections during the bull market (NYDIG). Each followed by 2–3 months of consolidation, then resumption — a cycle with high volatility but no single major crash.
- **May 2021 (the textbook case):** $64,000 → $30,000 (-53%), recovered in \~5 months to a new high of $69,000 in November. Bottom in late June, summer consolidation, resumption in September.
- **2013:** Included one >-70% correction mid-cycle, recovered within 7 months.

**Mid-cycle correction vs. cycle-ending (practical indicators):**

| Indicator | Bull market (mid-cycle correction) | Bear market (cycle-ending) |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly vs 200-week MA / Ichimoku cloud | Holds above | Breaks below |
| Short-term holder realized price (STH) | Price above it | Price below it |
| True Market Mean (active investor avg. cost) | Above | Below |
| 50-day vs 200-day MA | Bullish alignment | Death cross (2017 / 2021 top pattern) |

**Current state (2026-06):** Per Glassnode's Week 22 report, STH realized price has fallen below the True Market Mean (first time since January 2022), and price is below the True Market Mean (\~$78k) = late-stage bear market conditions flashing. This tilts toward the bear market camp.

## 4. Debate Over the Validity of the 4-Year Cycle

### Weakening / death camp (ETF · institutional)

- **Bitwise (Matt Hougan):** Declared the 4-year cycle "dead" in a 2025-12-26 memo. Reasons — (1) halving impact halves each cycle, (2) rates rose in 2018/2022 vs. expected to fall in 2026, (3) leverage reduced after the record liquidations of October 2025, (4) Morgan Stanley, Wells Fargo, and Merrill Lynch have begun offering BTC products to wealth-management clients (Bitwise estimates the traditional capital opening up at over $15 trillion). *Caveat: Hougan also told CNBC during the February 2026 drop that "the #1 reason for the current decline is the four-year cycle" — somewhat contradictory.*
- **CryptoQuant (Ki Young Ju):** "The 4-year cycle theory is dead — last cycle whales sold to retail, but this time old whales are selling to new long-term whales." *Caveat: simultaneously warns the bear market could last until early 2027 (verbatim above).*
- **Quantitative basis:** Amberdata — daily ETF demand is \~12x daily mined supply; record single-day net inflow of $324M in October 2025; ETPs held 6.4% of BTC circulating supply (\~1.3M BTC) as of January 2026.

### Still-valid camp

- **PlanB (S2F model, 2025-10-20, X, verbatim):** *"Bears think $126k was the top, and btc will fall below $100k, and 2026 will be a bear market mainly because … the 4 year cycle!? IMO that is a BIG misunderstanding... three cycles are not enough for a reliable pattern."* Average target this cycle $500,000 (range $250,000–$1,000,000). Caveat: S2F has badly missed in the past (failed 2021 $100k prediction; BTC fell to $15,500) — treat as estimate only.
- **Fidelity:** Maintains the 4-year cycle frame; notes every past bear market fell at least -77% but this one stopped at -52%. Jurrien Timmer says the cycle is *"playing out as expected"* and 2026 could be a weak year. Fidelity research separately flags that the 1-year realized volatility right after the ATH hit a record low for the 17th time (unprecedented in history).

### Neutral / synthesis

The evidence is genuinely mixed. The top timing (\~18 months after the halving) lines up perfectly with the 4-year cycle, but the shallow drawdown (-51% vs. past -77%+) suggests structural change.

## Caveats

- **Limited sample:** Only four completed cycles — statistically thin. No guarantee "the past repeats."
- **All future targets are estimates:** S2F, technical models, and prediction markets can all be wrong. PlanB's $500k comes from a model that badly missed last cycle (predicted $100k).
- **Macro dependence:** BTC is currently sensitive to rates (Fed holding 3.50–3.75%) and geopolitics (Middle East tension, oil \~$100). All past 200-week MA reclaims occurred during Fed easing — if tightening persists, historical support could break.
- **Structural change cuts both ways:** Bulls argue expanded ETF/institutional holdings could soften the old-style -80% drawdown (the current -51% may be evidence), but it's unproven. Conversely, forced selling by corporate treasuries (especially Strategy, now sitting on record unrealized losses) could be a new downside risk.
- **Source reliability:** Historical data (CoinGecko, Glassnode, NYDIG, Fidelity, CoinDesk) is high-confidence. Analyst forecasts (PlanB, Hougan, Ki Young Ju, Ali Martinez) are opinions/estimates, some sourced from social media posts.
