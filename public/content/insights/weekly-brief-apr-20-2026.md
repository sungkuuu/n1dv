## 11. Projects of the Week: Three Selected Projects

**Project 1: Kelp DAO (rsETH) — Epicenter of 2026's Largest DeFi Exploit**

Kelp DAO is an Ethereum restaking protocol that issues rsETH, a Liquid Restaking Token (LRT) built on EigenLayer. The **$293M** exploit on April 19 occurred through a vulnerability in its LayerZero-powered cross-chain bridge, where 116,500 rsETH (~18% of circulating supply) was drained. The attacker sent a crafted message to LayerZero's cross-chain messaging layer, tricking the bridge into treating it as a legitimate instruction ¹³ ¹⁴.

The protocol's emergency multisig pauser froze contracts 46 minutes after the drain, and two subsequent attack attempts (each targeting 40,000 rsETH, ~$100M) were detected and reverted. The attacker deposited stolen rsETH as collateral on Aave V3 to borrow wETH, creating cascading cross-protocol damage that drove Aave TVL down $6.6B and AAVE token down -16%.
